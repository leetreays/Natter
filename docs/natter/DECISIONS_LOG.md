# Natter — Decisions Log

This is a curated decision record, not a transcript.

## 2026 — Product principles consolidated

### Decision
Natter is a friendship-learning product, not a parental surveillance product.

### Consequences
- Children own their conversations.
- Parents understand relationships, not messages.
- Parent insights should operate at relationship/pattern level.
- Child message content should not be casually surfaced.

---

## 2026 — Connect · Protect · Grow adopted as core expression

### Decision
Use:

**Connect · Protect · Grow**

as a concise expression of Natter's purpose.

### Meaning
- Connect — belonging and friendship
- Protect — safety and reassurance
- Grow — communication maturity and independence

---

## 2026 — Digital Rite of Passage defined as platform graduation

### Decision
The Digital Rite of Passage is a child-level/end-of-platform graduation concept.

### Rejected interpretation
It is **not** a stage completed separately for every friendship.

### Reason
The feature is intended to demonstrate that Natter can be outgrown and is therefore aligned with trust rather than lock-in.

---

## 2026 — Relationship-level source of truth

### Decision
Move toward the friendship document as the single relationship-level source of truth.

### Reason
Avoid duplicated derived relationship state across the app and make parent/child experiences consume consistent relationship intelligence.

---

## 2026 — Parent Friendship Journey structure

### Decision
Use the following section sequence:

1. Relationship identity
2. Current Relationship
3. Journey So Far
4. Coming Up
5. Privacy Promise

### Key language
- "Built through kindness, repair and trust."
- "Your child's conversations remain private."
- "You understand relationships, not messages."

---

## 2026 — Original shield remains primary logo direction

### Decision
Retain the original N/shield concept as the preferred primary brand direction.

### Design constraints
- thinner/refined outline;
- balanced N strokes;
- subtle symbolism;
- navy remains important;
- three-dot meaning may be introduced without overpowering the mark.

---

## 2026 — Website as dusk-to-dawn narrative

### Decision
The main website should use a continuous visual journey from darkness toward daylight rather than a conventional feature-grid landing page.

### Reason
It communicates discovery, reassurance and growth in a way aligned with the Natter brand.

---

## 2026 — Holding page used during website build

### Decision
Route public site traffic to a branded holding page while the full narrative site is unfinished.

### State
Custom domain and redirect were confirmed working in August 2026.

---

## 2026 — Coaching/repair over punishment where safe

### Decision
The Kindness Engine should prefer coaching, rewrite and repair opportunities before blocking when risk allows.

### Constraint
Safeguarding needs can override the coaching ladder.
