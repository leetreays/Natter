# Natter — Legal & Compliance Workstream

**Not legal advice. Obtain qualified UK legal/privacy advice before launch.**

Natter is a children's/family product and should treat legal/privacy work as core product infrastructure.

## Ownership / IP

Workstream:

- establish clear ownership of source code;
- ensure contractor/contributor IP assignment where relevant;
- document ownership of brand assets;
- consider UK trademark protection for Natter/name/logo;
- keep domain ownership and company/project ownership aligned;
- maintain evidence of original design/product work;
- ensure third-party assets have appropriate licences.

Ideas alone are not generally protected in the same way as copyrightable expression, registered marks or contractual rights. Protect the actual assets, brand, code, documents and agreements.

## Children's data / privacy

Key areas to assess:

- UK GDPR;
- Data Protection Act 2018;
- ICO Children's Code / Age Appropriate Design Code;
- lawful basis for each processing purpose;
- age/consent model;
- parental responsibility/consent where applicable;
- transparency appropriate for children;
- data minimisation;
- profiling/behavioural intelligence;
- automated decision-making implications;
- retention;
- deletion;
- access requests;
- security.

## Required/likely documents

Work toward:

- Privacy Notice;
- child-friendly privacy explanation;
- Terms of Use;
- Parent/Guardian Terms where appropriate;
- Cookie Notice / consent mechanism where required;
- Data Protection Impact Assessment;
- data inventory / Record of Processing Activities as applicable;
- retention schedule;
- incident/breach response procedure;
- safeguarding/escalation policy;
- vendor/processor register;
- internal access policy;
- deletion/account closure procedure.

## Product-specific privacy questions

Natter should explicitly document:

- what message content is stored;
- what behavioural signals are derived;
- which signals parents see;
- what parents do **not** see;
- whether AI vendors process content;
- whether data trains third-party models;
- how long derived relationship data is retained;
- how a child can exercise privacy rights;
- what happens on graduation / Digital Rite of Passage.

## Safety

Clarify:

- moderation thresholds;
- escalation handling;
- emergency/high-risk scenarios;
- safeguarding roles;
- human review, if any;
- reporting/blocking;
- evidence retention where genuinely necessary;
- false-positive/appeal considerations.

## Website

Before public product launch, legal links should be easy to find and written accessibly.

Avoid using generic boilerplate that contradicts actual Firebase, analytics, moderation or AI data flows.

## Engineering rule

Do not add a new analytics, moderation, AI or data-processing vendor without reviewing:

1. data sent;
2. purpose;
3. legal basis;
4. retention;
5. sub-processors;
6. geographic transfers;
7. child-data terms;
8. security;
9. deletion;
10. privacy notice impact.
