# Natter — Parent Experience

## Purpose

The parent experience should provide reassurance and meaningful relationship understanding **without turning Natter into message surveillance**.

## Core privacy boundary

Parents should understand relationships, not messages.

Child message content should not be exposed simply because it is technically available in the backend.

## Parent Insights

Known signal types have included:

- blocked-word attempts;
- Quiet Hours attempts;
- targeting concerns;
- rewrite tracking;
- Protected Delivery-related information;
- behavioural summaries.

The goal is not to overwhelm parents with alerts. Signals should be synthesised into useful support.

## Friendship Journey

The parent Friendship Journey is relationship-level.

Established section order from the mid-2026 implementation:

1. Relationship identity
2. Current Relationship
3. Journey So Far
4. Coming Up / Looking Ahead
5. Privacy Promise

Established copy direction includes:

**Built through kindness, repair and trust.**

and a Privacy Promise centred on:

**Your child's conversations remain private.**

**You understand relationships, not messages.**

## Relationship identity

Historical implementation derives a friendship title from the two child names where available, e.g. `Name & Name`.

The friendship stage is read from relationship/friendship data and mapped through the Friendship Stage registry.

## Journey So Far

Meaningful Moments are displayed as a timeline rather than raw message excerpts.

## Coming Up

Future relationship stages may be surfaced as forward-looking guidance.

## Parent design principle

The parent product should feel calm, premium and reassuring rather than alarmist.

It should avoid red-alert aesthetics unless genuinely necessary for safeguarding.
