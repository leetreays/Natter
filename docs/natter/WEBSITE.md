# Natter — Website

## Domain

**getnatter.co.uk**

Purchased in July 2026.

## Purpose

A simple, trust-building information site for:

- parents;
- children;
- schools;
- future investors/partners.

It should explain Natter without feeling like a conventional feature-heavy SaaS website.

## Information architecture direction

Primary navigation:

- Story
- How it works
- Join the journey

Subtle secondary destinations may include:

- Schools
- Investors
- Legal

## Narrative

The site is conceived as a continuous scroll from dark night toward daylight.

This mirrors:

- beginning;
- discovery;
- connection;
- safety;
- growth.

Core sequence introduces:

**Connect · Protect · Grow**

## Scene direction snapshot

### Scene 1 — night / beginning

- very dark sky;
- subtle stars;
- three golden orbs;
- hidden/winding N-like path;
- central arrow;
- monochrome shield.

### Scene 2 — pre-dawn

- slightly brighter;
- moon/sun transition;
- orbs remain;
- shield remains predominantly white;
- minimal copy.

### Scene 3 — dawn / Connect

- one orb turns blue;
- title: `Connect`;
- simplified composition;
- friendship/belonging visual language.

### Scene 4 — early morning / Connect detail

Established copy direction:

**A place to belong. A place to begin.**

## Brand progression

The website shield/logo can evolve from monochrome to colour as the user moves through the narrative.

This gradual colour introduction is intentional and should not be replaced with a fully saturated brand treatment on the opening frame without a design decision.
